## 2. The problem being measured

The corpus is sharply bimodal. **68.1%** of reviews sit at the extremes (1–2 or 9–10), the corpus mean rating is **7.17/10**, and the outcome shares are **negative 22.9%**, **mixed 8.4%**, **positive 68.8%**. A retriever that ranks by similarity alone inherits and amplifies that shape, because emphatic reviews are also the ones most lexically similar to an emphatic question.

Two metrics quantify the resulting distortion, both defined in `pharos/retrieval/stratified.py`:

- **VSD** (Valence Skew Divergence) — Jensen–Shannon divergence, in bits, between the retrieved panel's outcome distribution and the cohort's true one. 0 is a perfectly representative panel.
- **CRE** (Cohort Rating Error) — absolute difference between the panel's mean rating and the cohort's, on the 1–10 scale. How many stars a reader of the panel alone would be misled by.

## 4. Reading these numbers

Against `naive-baseline` -- dense top-k with no fusion, no redundancy control and no stratification, which is what a conventional RAG retriever does -- the full system reduces valence skew from **0.0324** to **0.0082** — a **75%** reduction — and cohort rating error from **0.667** to **0.377** stars, while nDCG@10 moves from 0.8382 to 0.7858.

That trade is the finding. Representativeness is not free: reserving panel slots for minority outcomes means admitting units that rank slightly lower on relevance. The claim this project makes is that the trade is worth taking for a summarisation task, because a panel that misrepresents its cohort produces a confidently wrong answer, and a panel with marginally lower nDCG produces a slightly less on-topic one. Those failures are not equivalent, and only one of them is visible to the reader.


**What is not claimed.** The gold set derives relevance from corpus metadata (`pharos/eval/goldset.py`), so it measures whether retrieval finds units matching a query's structured intent — a necessary condition for good retrieval, not a sufficient one. Aspect labels come from a deterministic weak labeller and carry its error rate. Nothing here measures answer quality as a clinician would judge it, and no statistic computed over self-selected reviews is a pharmacovigilance signal.


## 5. Reproducing

```bash
pharos fetch-data
pharos build-corpus
pharos build-index
pharos evaluate      # regenerates this file
```

No API key and no model download are required for any of the above: the default encoder is fitted on the corpus itself, and every metric in this document is computed without a generative model.
